      <h2>What are cookies?</h2>
      <p>Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site's owners.</p>
      <p>Cookies can be “persistent” or “session” cookies. Persistent cookies remain on your device for a period set in the cookie and are activated each time you visit the website that created the cookie. Session cookies are temporary and expire at the end of your browsing session.</p>

      <h2>How we use cookies</h2>
      <p>Dynasty Nova uses different types of cookies to improve your experience on our site and in our game:</p>

      <h3>Essential cookies</h3>
      <p>These cookies are necessary for our site to work and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set in response to actions you take that amount to a request for services, such as logging in to your account or filling in forms.</p>
      <p>On our site, these cookies are mainly used to:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Keep you logged in</li>
        <li>Temporarily save guest account information</li>
        <li>Enable the game's basic features</li>
        <li>Keep the site secure</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>Non-essential cookies</h3>
      <p>These cookies improve your experience on our site but are not essential to its operation. They may include cookies for analytics, personalization and possibly, in the future, advertising.</p>
      <p>We do not use any non-essential cookies yet, but once we implement them, they could cover the following functions:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Analyzing how users interact with our site</li>
        <li>Remembering your preferences (language, region, game settings)</li>
        <li>Personalizing your experience</li>
        <li>Showing relevant ads (through Google AdSense, for example)</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>How to manage your cookies</h2>
      <p>Most web browsers let you control cookies through their settings. Here is how to manage cookies in the most popular browsers:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Google Chrome</strong>: Menu → Settings → Show advanced settings → Privacy → Content settings → Cookies</li>
        <li><strong>Firefox</strong>: Menu → Options → Privacy → History → Use custom settings for history → Cookies</li>
        <li><strong>Safari</strong>: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data</li>
        <li><strong>Microsoft Edge</strong>: Menu → Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies</li>
      </ul>
      <p>Please note that restricting cookies may prevent you from using some features of our site. In particular, essential cookies are required for you to browse the site and use its features, such as accessing its secure areas.</p>

      <h2>Third-party cookies</h2>
      <p>In addition to our own cookies, we may also use various third-party cookies to report site usage statistics, deliver ads and so on. These cookies fall into the following categories:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Analytics services (coming soon)</li>
        <li>Advertising services (such as Google AdSense, coming soon)</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Updates to our cookie policy</h2>
      <p>We may update this cookie policy from time to time. Changes take effect as soon as they are posted on this page. We encourage you to check this page regularly to stay up to date on how we use cookies.</p>

      <h2>Contact us</h2>
      <p>If you have any questions about our use of cookies, feel free to contact us through the <a href="/en/contact/">Contact</a> page.</p>
