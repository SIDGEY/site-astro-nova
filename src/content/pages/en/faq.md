      - question: "What is Dynasty Nova?"
        answer: "Dynasty Nova is a 4X space strategy game where you build your own interstellar empire: manage your resources, research technologies, grow your fleet, and explore, conquer and dominate the galaxy."
      - question: "What is a dynasty in Dynasty Nova?"
        answer: "It's your people of origin, chosen in each universe once and for all. The Heritage brings together humans, the augmented, cyborgs and clones (“No one gets left behind”); the Accord unites xenos of many species (“Attune, or tremble”). Each dynasty brings two traits active from the very first minute and two classes that belong to it alone, each with its own talent tree. A third dynasty, the Choir, is not playable yet."
      - question: "Is Dynasty Nova free?"
        answer: "Yes, Dynasty Nova is free to play, with no cap on progression. The game follows a Pay-to-Fast model rather than Pay-to-Win: no purchase gives you a lasting strategic advantage, only an optional way to save time."
      - question: "Which platforms can I play Dynasty Nova on?"
        answer: "Dynasty Nova is a browser game: there's nothing to download. You play it straight from your web browser, on a computer or a phone."
      - question: "Is Dynasty Nova like OGame?"
        answer: "Dynasty Nova keeps the fundamentals that made OGame a success (planet management, technology research, fleets, colonization) and pairs them with a modern interface, your planet in 3D and a tech tree reorganized by category."
      - question: "How do I play Dynasty Nova?"
