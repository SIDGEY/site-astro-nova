## Can you play a space strategy game on mobile without an app?

Yes: **Dynasty Nova runs in your phone's browser**, with no app store and no download. Just open play.dynastynova.com in Safari or Chrome and log in. You can also add the game to your home screen, where it opens full screen like an app.

### Why there's no app to download

A browser game updates on the server side: no updates to install, no different version depending on your phone, and the same game everywhere. It's a direct legacy of games like OGame, with an interface designed from the start for a phone screen.

### What you need to play

A recent browser and a connection are all it takes. There's nothing to install to get started, and you can create your account right from your phone.

## Add the game to your home screen

Adding the game to your home screen is optional, but it gives you a dedicated icon and a full-screen view, without the browser's address bar.

### On iPhone and iPad

Open play.dynastynova.com in **Safari**, tap the **Share** button, then **“Add to Home Screen”**. The Dynasty Nova icon appears alongside your other apps.

### On Android

Open play.dynastynova.com in **Chrome**, open the menu (the three dots at the top right), then choose **“Install app”** or **“Add to Home screen”**, depending on your version of Chrome.

## An interface built for your thumb

Dynasty Nova's interface was designed mobile-first: every screen is drawn for a phone first, then widened for desktop.

### The bottom bar

Your planet button always takes you back to the heart of your empire, and the bottom bar leads to your **Buildings**, **Research**, **Docks** and **Missions**. The four resources (metal, crystal, hydrogen, energy) stay visible at the top, updated live.

### The same information as on desktop

The tutorial, quests, alliance chat and notification center work the same way on a phone. On a large computer screen, the interface simply expands to show more information at once.

## Playing in short sessions

A browser space strategy game fits around your breaks, not long evenings in front of a screen.

### The game moves on without you

Construction, research and fleet trips carry on while your phone is asleep. Starting a construction queue in the morning and finding it finished by lunchtime is part of the game.

### Leave without losing your empire

For a real break, **vacation mode** pauses your empire in a universe: production drops to zero and nobody can reach you, for at least 48 hours. You can only leave once your fleets are home and your construction work is finished.
