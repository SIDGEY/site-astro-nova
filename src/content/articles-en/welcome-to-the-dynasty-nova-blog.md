## Welcome, Commanders!

**Dynasty Nova is a free space strategy game you play in your browser**, on desktop or mobile, with nothing to download. This blog is its logbook: here we tell the story of how the game is being built, from the first lines of code to the battles being fought in the galaxy today.

### What is Dynasty Nova?

In Dynasty Nova, each player starts from one planet and builds an interstellar empire by managing their resources, technologies and fleets, in a galaxy shared with other players. The game belongs to the family of space management browser games made popular by OGame: three resources (metal, crystal, hydrogen), buildings to level up, a research tree, ships and defenses. The full picture is in [our overview of the game](/en/blog/free-space-strategy-browser-game-dynasty-nova/).

### Where should you start if you're new to the game?

The first hours set the pace for an entire empire: which mines to build, when to start research, how to make the most of newcomer protection. Our [guide to your first hours](/en/blog/space-strategy-game-beginners-guide-first-hours/) walks you through them step by step.

## What you'll find on this blog

### Development diaries

The project's technical and artistic challenges, and what's new in each update, told from the player's point of view.

### Gameplay explainers

The game's mechanics in detail: combat, technologies, resource management, alliance life.

### Community news

Server openings and the events we share with players on Discord.

## Why this blog?

### Building the game with its players

Developing a game of this scope is a human adventure. We want to break the ice between developers and players: your feedback is essential to making Dynasty Nova a benchmark for space strategy games.

> "The universe isn't a limit, it's a playground."

### Joining the discussion

Our [Discord](https://discord.gg/dVXdVqw5d5) is the place to talk about each article and share your ideas for where the game goes next. Space is vast, but we're not alone in this adventure.
