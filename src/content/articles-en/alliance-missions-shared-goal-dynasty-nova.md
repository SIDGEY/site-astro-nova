## Introduction

In **Dynasty Nova**, a free space strategy game you play in your browser, on desktop or mobile, an alliance does more than share a chat channel. It sets itself **missions**: plundering together, exploring, recycling, resupplying the treasury. We've just rebuilt alliance missions around a simple idea: everyone knows what they need to do, and everyone is rewarded for what they did.

## How an alliance mission works

### A daily board and a weekly board

The alliance's Missions tab holds two boards: **Today's mission**, drawn every day, and **This week's missions**, drawn every week. Each draw offers missions from five families: **Raid**, **Exploration**, **Territory**, **Coordination** and **Challenge**. Only one mission per family can run at a time.

### Three difficulties to unlock

Each proposal displays its difficulty: **Standard**, **Advanced** or **Epic**. The last two unlock as the alliance takes its [alliance station](/en/blog/dynasty-nova-alliance-station-doctrine-tree/), its collective investment, up through the tiers. Each proposal also shows what the player viewing it can expect to earn.

## Enroll before the objective is set

### Only enrolled members count

A chosen mission first opens for enrollment, with a countdown. The screen puts it plainly: “Only enrolled members count and share the reward.” Enrolling takes a single button, **Enroll**. Once the mission has started, an enrolled member sees that their actions already count.

### An objective sized to the team that enrolled

A mission's objective isn't the same for every alliance: it's set from the enrolled members' production, measured when enrollment closes. Until then, the card shows an **estimated target** based on the members enrolled so far, so everyone knows what they're signing up for. The objective follows the real size of the team taking it on.

## See your own share, not just the alliance's

### The “Your share” box

The alliance bar shows collective progress, and it keeps going past 100%: beating the objective increases the reward. Below that bar, every enrolled member now sees **their own share**: where they stand, a bar, and what's left to do, for example “4.8k more resources to deliver to the treasury by convoy.” Depending on the mission, that share is sized to the member's own production or split equally among the enrolled members.

### Who has done their share

The list of enrolled members shows a bar for each member, and a green check mark once their share is done. The summary tells you how many enrolled members have completed theirs. Officers also have a right to **bar a member from missions**: that member no longer counts and gets no share, and the decision is recorded in the alliance journal.

## Claim your reward

### The outcome of yesterday's mission

A mission is settled when its cycle ends. So that a success doesn't vanish from the screen the very moment it ends, each board shows the outcome of the previous cycle: **Yesterday's mission** or **Last week's missions**, success or objective not reached, progress, and the experience the alliance earned.

### Delivered by the Stellar Station

Below that outcome, your share is waiting with the **Claim** button. You choose the delivery planet, and the Stellar Station ships it there: the share shows “On its way,” then “Delivered.” If you don't claim it in time, it's delivered automatically to your mother planet. If you received nothing, the screen tells you why, such as a missed enrollment.
