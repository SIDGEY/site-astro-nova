## Introduction: bringing a space game legend back to life

Dynasty Nova started out as a closed beta project designed to “breathe new life into the genre while adapting it to modern habits, a smoother experience and player-centered design.” The developer, who is passionate about video games and interactive experiences, frames the project as both a tribute and an experimental lab, favoring a UX-first methodology in which every planet becomes a living, evolving world.

## Background: OGame's legacy and the search for a peaceful game

### OGame's revolutionary impact

In the early 2000s, OGame introduced a revolutionary concept: building persistent space empires in a web browser. Its mix of resource management, colonization, alliances and real-time strategic battles made it the benchmark of the PBBG (Persistent Browser-Based Game) genre.

### Answering players' frustration

For all its appeal, OGame came with constant tension. Players who preferred building and managing kept running into repeated attacks. That observation planted the seed of Dynasty Nova's core concept: “a calmer, more personal, more immersive space strategy game.”

## Dynasty Nova's three founding pillars

1. A smart solo mode with adaptive bots that adjust the difficulty level
2. An immersive 3D interface with animated planets and natural interactions
3. Universal accessibility, playable straight from a web browser on desktop and mobile

## A planet-centered design

Instead of static data tables, Dynasty Nova puts the planet at the heart of the experience. Inspired by the look of interactive globes (GitHub Universe style), each planet slowly rotates, reacts to lighting and evolves with the player's actions.

### Design philosophy

The developer applies core modern UX principles:
- Critical information readable at a glance
- Modular navigation (side menu, dynamic planet, central content)
- Constant feedback that makes every action easier to understand

The visual style blends minimalist pixel art with a contemporary low-poly look, with technical typography, subtle lighting effects and dark tones that create immersive yet approachable atmospheres.

## Technical architecture

- **Frontend:** React (JavaScript) for modularity
- **3D rendering:** WebGL/Three.js for interactive planets
- **Backend:** JSON + local storage for the testing phase
- **Future:** API infrastructure and database planned for the public release

The lightweight hosting approach favors responsiveness and user-driven evolution.

## What sets Dynasty Nova apart from OGame clones

Dynasty Nova stands out through:
- A peaceful, introspective vision of the genre
- An immersive, ergonomic design that puts visual comfort first
- True cross-platform accessibility, with no installation required

## Accessibility and target audience

The game is aimed at players looking for solo space strategy, Android alternatives to OGame, or gentler web experiences: a new generation of players who prefer building to competing.
