## Why defend your planet?

In a browser-based space strategy game like Dynasty Nova, free and playable on mobile, **a planet can still be attacked while its owner is offline**. The fleet may be out on a mission, the player asleep: at that point, only fixed defenses respond. Their first job isn't to win every battle, but to make an attack too costly to be worth the trip.

### Defending means deterring

A careful attacker starts by spying. In Dynasty Nova, a spy report reveals defenses from tier 5 up, and the attacker then tests the strike in the [battle simulator](/en/blog/battle-simulator-test-your-fleet-dynasty-nova/). A visible, solid defense often sends them looking for an easier target.

### A defense never leaves on a mission

Unlike ships, a defense stays on the planet where it was built. So it protects the base even when your entire fleet is flying to the far side of the galaxy.

## Dynasty Nova's defenses

The Defense screen sorts ten units into two categories, **Standard Defenses** and **Advanced Defenses**.

### Turrets

Six turrets fire during combat rounds: the Ballistic Projector, Photonic Cannon, High-Energy Emitter, Magnetic Accelerator, Ion Battery and Plasma Ejector. The first few are simple and cost-effective, the Photonic Cannon works well against small ships and fighters, and the Plasma Ejector brings up the rear. A varied line holds up better than a single turret type stacked high.

### Planetary shields

The Defensive Barrier and the Protective Dome absorb a large amount of damage. Each one is **unique**: only one per base.

### The research that counts

Three technologies strengthen ships and defenses alike: **Weapon Systems** for weapons, **Shielding Technology** for shields, **Armor Technology** for hulls. Leveling them up improves your whole defense at once.

## Missiles: intercept and strike back

Missiles are stored in a **missile silo**, whose capacity is counted in slots.

### Interceptors

An opponent can target your defenses with Long-Range Warheads. A salvo gives no warning before impact, but it only destroys defenses, never buildings, the fleet or resources. Each Interceptor in stock shoots down one warhead, automatically.

### Long-Range Warheads

Warheads are used to weaken a target's defenses before an attack. They stay within the same galaxy, and their range depends on a propulsion technology. A strike counts as an attack: the protections below apply to it too.

## Protections on top of your defenses

Dynasty Nova doesn't leave new players to face veterans alone.

### The first days

Every player gets [7 days of protection](/en/blog/new-player-protection-seven-day-shield-dynasty-nova/) on arriving in a universe. After that, a protection based on the point gap between players takes over, and a quota limits the number of attacks on the same target.

### Time away

For a planned absence, [vacation mode](/en/blog/vacation-mode-protect-your-empire-dynasty-nova/) shelters your empire, one universe at a time. A [moon](/en/blog/moons-space-strategy-game-dynasty-nova/) can also hold its own defenses.

## Good habits

Defenses aren't everything: the best protection is still a stockpile that tempts no one.

### Spend before you log off

Plundering only carries off part of your stock, but a big stockpile is still a target. Starting a build or a research before you go cuts down what a raid can take.
