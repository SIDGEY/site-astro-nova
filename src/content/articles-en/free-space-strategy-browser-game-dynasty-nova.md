## What is Dynasty Nova?

**Dynasty Nova is a free space strategy game you play in your browser**, on desktop or mobile, with nothing to download. Each player starts from a single planet and builds an interstellar empire by managing resources, technologies and fleets, in a galaxy shared with other players. The first official server opened on September 26, 2026.

### An heir to OGame, by design

Dynasty Nova belongs to the family of space management browser games that OGame made popular: three resources (metal, crystal, hydrogen), buildings to level up, a research tree, ships and defenses. We kept what gives the genre its depth and rethought the interface, the way beginners are welcomed and the business model. The details are in [what changes for a player used to OGame](/en/blog/game-like-ogame-what-changes-in-dynasty-nova/).

### What you do in the game

- **Produce**: mines, energy and storage, the engine of any empire.
- **Research**: technologies that unlock buildings, ships and defenses.
- **Build a fleet**: combat ships, transports, spy probes.
- **Explore and colonize**: expeditions, new planets, moons in orbit.
- **Ally or fight**: alliances, pacts, wars decided by vote.

### A game that welcomes latecomers

The biggest hurdle in games of this genre is arriving after everyone else. In Dynasty Nova, every player is [protected for **7 days**](/en/blog/new-player-protection-seven-day-shield-dynasty-nova/) from their first arrival in a universe, and only loses that shield by attacking someone themselves. Protection based on the point gap between players rounds out the system.

### Games with friends

A group of friends can join a [private universe with an invitation code](/en/blog/private-universe-invite-code-dynasty-nova/) and play among themselves, under the same rules as the public universes.

## What Dynasty Nova is not

A clear promise beats a broad one. Here is what you won't find.

### No pay-to-win

The shop sells avatars and planet skins. The Premium subscription adds convenience: longer construction queues, one extra expedition and a monthly set of exclusive items. No purchase decides the outcome of a battle. We explain this in [Pay-to-fast, not pay-to-win](/en/blog/dynasty-nova-pay-to-fast-not-pay-to-win/).

### Not a game that demands you stay online all the time

Dynasty Nova is played in short sessions: start a construction, send a fleet, reply to your alliance. Vacation mode protects your empire while you're away, universe by universe.

## Where to start?

The simplest way is to create an account, join an open universe and follow the tutorial, which guides your first constructions without blocking the screen. Our [guide to your first hours](/en/blog/space-strategy-game-beginners-guide-first-hours/) walks through the order of decisions.

### Your first moves

Level up your three mines evenly, keep an eye on energy, then join an alliance before your week of protection runs out. Exploring the galaxy starts at [play.dynastynova.com](https://play.dynastynova.com/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=pilier-strategie-spatiale).
