## Introduction

Dynasty Nova is a free space strategy game you play in your browser, on desktop or mobile. Your planet is shown in 3D, and it has just been given a new look: a drifting **cloud layer**, **shadows** cast on the ground and an **atmospheric haze** around its edge. Here's what you see now, biome by biome.

## New surfaces for every world

### Seven biomes rendered in 3D

Every planet in Dynasty Nova belongs to a **biome**: temperate, ocean, jungle, desert, arid, ice or gas giant. Each one gets a new generation of textures, which sets the color of the ground, its relief and the way it reflects sunlight.

### Moons too

The six **moon** variants get the same surface update. A moon does keep its look of an airless body, though: no clouds, no haze, as described in [our article on their new look](/en/blog/dynasty-nova-moons-get-their-own-look/).

## A cloud layer above the ground

### Six biomes under the clouds

Temperate, ocean, jungle, desert, arid and ice planets now carry clouds, set on a thin layer just above the ground. The **gas giant** is the exception: its colored bands already play the part of clouds.

### Light that follows the sun

The clouds are lit by the same sun as the planet. Along the line between day and night, they pick up a warm fringe; at the edge of the sphere, they take on the color of the planet's halo.

### Drifting clouds

The clouds don't rotate as a single block with the ground: they drift a little faster. The offset is strong enough for your eye to see the layer slide as the planet spins.

## Shadows and atmosphere

### Shadows cast on the ground

Each cloud casts its **shadow** on the surface, offset away from the sun. That way, the relief of continents and oceans still reads beneath the cloud cover instead of simply being hidden.

### A haze that thickens toward the edge

An **atmospheric haze** covers the ground and thickens toward the planet's edge, like an atmosphere seen from space. Its color depends on the world: bluish for a temperate planet, green for a jungle, ocher for a desert. The gas giant has one too.

## Where to see these planets

### In the game

The 3D planet appears on the game's Planet page, for each of your worlds. To place your world among its neighbors, the [orbital view](/en/blog/dynasty-nova-orbital-view/) shows each system with its star and orbiting bodies.

### On our home page

The same planets spin on our [home page](/en/#planets), rendered by the same engine as the game: you can switch from one biome to another and rotate the sphere before you even create an account.
