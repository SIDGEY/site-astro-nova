## Why a space strategy game glossary?

Browser space strategy games like OGame and Dynasty Nova have a vocabulary of their own, built up over twenty years of play and forum threads. This glossary defines the terms new players run into most often, with their exact meaning in Dynasty Nova.

### How to read it

Each definition stands on its own. The terms are grouped by theme: the economy, the fleet, protections, then life in an alliance.

## Economy and galaxy

- **Universe**: a game server, with its own players, ranking and settings. One account can play in several universes.
- **Universe speed**: the multiplier that sets a universe's pace. At x1 speed, durations are the baseline ones; a faster universe shortens them.
- **Metal, crystal, hydrogen**: the three construction resources. Hydrogen also serves as fuel for fleets.
- **Energy**: the resource that keeps the mines running, produced by Photovoltaic Sensors and later by the Thermonuclear Reactor.
- **Storage**: the planet's capacity for each resource. When storage for a resource is full, its production pauses.
- **Colony**: an additional planet, gained through the colonization mission.

## Fleet and combat

- **Espionage probe**: the most affordable ship, which reports what a planet holds. The deeper the report, the more it reveals: the fleet, then the defenses, then the research.
- **Raid**: an attack launched to plunder another planet's resources.
- **Battle simulator**: the tool that replays a battle 50 times with the game's real engine, based on a spy report, and gives you a win rate.
- **Debris field**: the remains of the ships destroyed in a battle, left in orbit.
- **Recycling**: the mission that collects the resources of a debris field.
- **Stationing**: the mission that moves a fleet to another of your planets and leaves it there.
- **Expedition**: a mission into the unknown, with variable outcomes.
- **Interplanetary missiles**: warheads fired from the Ballistic Arsenal, a long-range strike that doesn't commit a fleet. The defender's interceptors can destroy them before impact.

## Moons

- **Moon**: a celestial body that can form in orbit after a battle, from the debris. It has its own buildings.
- **Sensor phalanx**: a moon building whose paid sweep reveals the composition and timing of an enemy fleet.
- **Jump gate**: a moon building that instantly transfers a fleet between two of your moons, with a cooldown.

## Protections

- **Newcomer protection**: in Dynasty Nova, a 7-day shield against attacks from the moment you arrive in a universe, lost if you launch an attack yourself.
- **Beginner protection**: a rule that forbids attacks between two players whose point gap is too wide, in both directions.
- **Bashing**: attacking the same player many times in a short period. **Anti-bashing** limits these attacks with a quota on any one player, all of their colonies combined.
- **Vacation mode**: a pause of at least 48 hours in a universe, with production at zero and an empire that can't be attacked.

## Alliances

- **Alliance**: a group of players with its own station, common treasury and shared missions.
- **Pact**: an agreement between two alliances, with a betrayal cost and a notice period for breaking it.
- **Alliance war**: a conflict declared by a vote of the members, with a score you can follow live.
