## Introduction

A building costs less than expected, but why? In a growing empire, talents, plans and orders stack up until the price becomes impossible to read. Dynasty Nova now shows **the cost reduction applied to every building and every research project**, along with where it comes from. Mobile sheets also get collapsible blocks.

## See the discount at a glance

### A badge next to the level

The **green badge** tells you that a price carries a discount. It sits next to the level, in the header of a building or research sheet, and gives the total percentage. Before you even read the cost, you know your empire is working in your favor.

### A line under the cost

Under the cost sits a collapsible line: “This building gets a …% discount,” or “This research” for a technology. It states that the discount is **already taken off the cost shown**. The price you read is the price you'll pay.

## Where does the discount come from?

### The breakdown, source by source

Expanded, the line lists each source with its share in a green tag. For buildings, the sheet can list:

- **talents** tied to building cost;
- **terraformer** and **lunar base** talents;
- the **Known plan**, when a level has already been reached elsewhere;
- **Small world**;
- the **State order**.

For research, you'll find talents on research cost and on high research levels, the **level known in the universe** and **reverse engineering**.

### When percentages don't add up

Two discounts don't always add up. When the State order comes on top of the others, it applies to the already reduced price. The sheet spells it out in black and white: the discounts do not add up. When you're building an empire strategy, it's better to know the rule than to guess it.

### Prices that update after a talent change

Reallocating your talent points also refreshes the building and research lists. The displayed price follows your tree, without waiting for the old price to go away on its own.

## Sheets that collapse on mobile

### Requirements and Unlocks

On a screen narrower than 768 px, the sheet is one long column. The **Requirements** and **Unlocks** blocks become collapsible there, which makes the screen easier to read on the way to the cost and the action. The Requirements block shows a counter (conditions met out of the total). It stays open as long as a condition is missing, and collapses once everything is met.

### Nothing changes on large screens

From 768 px up, nothing collapses: the blocks display as before. This care for small screens extends Dynasty Nova's [mobile-first](/en/blog/dynasty-nova-mobile-first-ui-design/) approach.
