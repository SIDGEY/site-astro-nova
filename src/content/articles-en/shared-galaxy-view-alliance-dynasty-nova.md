## Introduction

Should a position a teammate has spied stay a mystery to the rest of the fleet? In Dynasty Nova, it doesn't. The **shared galaxy view** passes discoveries around within an [alliance](/en/blog/dynasty-nova-alliances/), and safeguards protect every member from an unfortunate shot.

## A map the whole alliance lights up

### What is shared vision?

**Shared vision** makes a position that a single member has spied visible to the whole alliance, with no one else needing to send a probe. What gets shared is the **map status**, never the report: no resources, no defenses, no fleet, no date.

### What happens to your earlier discoveries?

Whether you join an alliance or found one, the positions you had already spied are added to the alliance's. Nothing is overwritten: everyone's discoveries add up.

### What does this vision unlock?

A position seen by the alliance opens up **colonization** and **recycling**, just as a report of your own would. Quick espionage is still offered: since you have no report of your own, scouting it again still makes sense.

## Knowing where your intel comes from

### A badge that shows the source

An “alliance discovery” badge is pinned to the planet's avatar, in both list and grid views. It keeps you from thinking you hold a report you don't have.

### A panel that says it plainly

The planet's panel reads “Discovered by an alliance member”, with no date, since none is shared. The dated “Spy Report” line is reserved for positions you probed yourself, and if both exist, yours takes precedence.

## No friendly fire

### Spot an ally at a glance

In the galaxy, an ally's username shows in **emerald**, with a handshake badge. Their panel says “Member of your alliance”.

### Attack grayed out, with the reason why

On an ally's planet, the **Attack** tile is grayed out and the tooltip explains: “No friendly fire”. The missile strike tile disappears, and firing is still refused when you aim by coordinates from the Fleet page. It's not just a display rule: sending a fleet and launching missiles are refused as well.

### What is still allowed

Only aggression is blocked. Espionage, transport and recycling remain open between allies, and some of them are precisely the point of being allies.

## Share a report, a battle, a position

### A Share button

The detail view of a **spy report** or a **battle report** has a Share button, with an optional comment. The report goes to the alliance channel, which the [alliance chat](/en/blog/alliance-chat-coordinates-spy-reports-dynasty-nova/) brings to life. Transport arrivals, fleet returns and intrusion alerts can't be shared: they're about what happens to your own bases.

### Everyone opens it with their own rights

Every member, current and future, can open the report from the channel. The message only carries a link: the share disappears along with the report, and stays in the channel for 30 days. A member who leaves the alliance loses access. A borrowed report says so with a banner and can't be deleted or shared again, but “Simulate this battle” and “Send a fleet” are still offered.

### Share a coordinate

A galaxy cell's panel offers “Share X:Y:Z with the alliance”, even on a cell nobody has ever spied. The link opens each member's galaxy view at the right position.

## Conclusion

Shared vision turns every probe into collective intelligence, and with friendly fire ruled out, the alliance can focus its strength on its rivals. Exploring the galaxy together starts on [play.dynastynova.com](https://play.dynastynova.com/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=vision-alliance), where your next discovery will light up the map for your whole alliance.
