## Introduction

Running a space empire means moving smoothly between your shop, your planets, your ranking and your profile. A recent update reworks these four screens in depth, with a single goal: every page should put what matters for your strategy first.

## A simpler shop: Credits and Avatars

### Two tabs, one clear promise

The shop drops its Resources and Cosmetics tabs in favor of two clear entries: **Credits**, for Stellar Points packs, and **Avatars**, which hosts the portrait gallery directly.

### Redesigned avatars

Avatar cards now take the shape of round medallions, with their price shown only once, at the level of their rarity group, rather than repeated on every card.

## A planet page built around your inventories

### Every inventory in its place

No more scattered sections: each inventory (buildings with resources and installations kept separate, Innovation Center, defenses, ships) now scrolls horizontally, with the level or quantity visible right on the card. Players land on the section where they can act on what they just read, instead of having to hunt for the action elsewhere on the page.

### Military strength travels with the inventory

Total military strength, previously isolated in a separate block, now travels with the inventory that produces it: a freshly founded colony therefore displays its stocks correctly, even before it has a single defense.

## A clearer ranking and separate settings

### A leaner ranking

Category tabs (technology, fleet, defense…) move into the ranking page header, just like on the buildings and ships pages. The triple population filter (All, Humans, Bots) becomes a single **Humans** button, which frees up screen space. Pagination stays visible at all times, pinned just above the navigation bar.

### Profile and account, finally separate

Username, email, password and newsletter sign-up leave the profile page for a new **Account** tab in Settings. The profile now focuses on the essentials: portrait, avatar selection and billing.

## Conclusion

None of these changes affects game balance: they simply cut the number of clicks and menus between you and the information you need to make a decision. A clearer planet page means faster building and defense decisions; a cleaner ranking means a rivalry you can follow without effort.
