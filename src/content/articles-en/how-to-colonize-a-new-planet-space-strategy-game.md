## Introduction

In a space strategy game, a single planet always ends up running out of room: fields, resources, options. **Colonizing** means founding a new base on a free planet. Here is how it works in Dynasty Nova, from the research to the first building.

## Unlocking a colony slot

### One planet to start, more with research

Dynasty Nova gives you **one planet** to start with. To own more, you need to research **Astrophysics**. It unlocks a new colony slot at levels **1, 3, 5, 7** and so on: two planets at level 1, three at level 3, four at level 5.

### A cap that's checked twice

The limit is checked when the fleet leaves, then again when it arrives. If you've reached it, the game shows “Colony limit reached” and the fleet returns to its base.

## Preparing the mission

### A colony ship in the fleet

A **Colonize** mission requires at least one **colony ship** in the fleet. To build one, you need an **Orbital Dock** at level 4, **Impulse Drive** at level 3 and **Astrophysics** at level 1. Its base cost is 10,000 metal, 20,000 crystal and 10,000 hydrogen. The ship is used up in founding the base. The other ships in the fleet stay on site and join the new colony's garrison. A talent can lower the cost of this ship.

### Spy first

You can't colonize a planet without having **spied** on it first. Espionage by a member of your alliance counts too: a position discovered by one member is open to the whole alliance. If you're just starting out, the [guide to your first hours](/en/blog/space-strategy-game-beginners-guide-first-hours/) explains how to lay the groundwork before aiming further.

## When the fleet arrives

### A one-way trip

Colonization is a **one-way trip**: you can't recall the fleet, it stays on site to found the base. Choose your target carefully before you send it, because there's no coming back.

### A report that tells you everything

On arrival, a colonization report says “Colony established” or “Colonization failed”. You can turn off success reports in your settings, but a failure is always reported. If the colony limit has been reached when the fleet arrives, it returns to its base; if the planet was taken in the meantime, the report says so too. On a free planet, the colony starts with the resources lying on that position plus the fleet's cargo, which is why choosing your target well pays off. [Cosmic events](/en/blog/meteorites-comets-giant-impacts-cosmic-events-dynasty-nova/) can drop resources there.

## Bringing your new colony to life

### The Colonial Base first

A brand-new colony needs its **Colonial Base**: smaller than the Planetary Base on your home world, it links the colony to your empire. Without it, no other construction or research is possible there.

### A first step that counts

Dynasty Nova even turns it into a quest, “First colony”: send your ship to a free planet and found the base. After that, each colony adds a planet to develop, and territory to defend.

## Conclusion

Colonizing comes down to four moves: research Astrophysics, build a colony ship at the Orbital Dock, spy on the target, then send the fleet on its one-way trip.
