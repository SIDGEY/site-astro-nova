## Introduction

On September 26, 2026, Dynasty Nova opens its first official server: the beta is coming to an end and a new universe begins, where every commander lays their first stone at the same moment as everyone else.

## Why September 26 marks the end of the beta

### A new universe, not one more beta

The official server isn't a new test cycle: it's the persistent universe Dynasty Nova will live on. Unlike the beta universes, opened and then closed with each wave of testing, this one stays open, and every empire built there counts for real.

### A full audit before the green light

Before setting the date, the team put the game through a full frontend audit, fixed four features that had silently regressed, tightened the quality gates in continuous integration, and removed more than 1,200 lines of dead code. The load time before the first route renders was also cut in half. Finishing that cleanup is what made it possible to announce September 26.

## What the beta helped fine-tune

### Balancing reviewed one mechanic at a time

Fleet speed can now be set to the exact percent instead of in steps, each game mechanic (research, construction, fleet) gets its own universe speed multiplier, and a battle can be simulated before committing a single fleet. On the shop side, paid speed-ups remain off-limits in fights between players, so competition stays a matter of strategy, not of wallets.

### Content built continuously

Alliances arrived with their doctrine tree, common purse, war votes and non-aggression pacts; moons now form in orbit around the planets they accompany; and founding an alliance no longer costs anything, so the first commanders can get organized at launch without waiting to build up resources.

## What day-one players get

### An empire that starts from zero, for everyone

A server that's just opening is an empty server: no galaxy already mapped, no alliance already settled in, no fleet already in position. Joining on September 26 means playing the same first hours as every other commander, with no established empire to catch up with.

### Found an alliance right away

Since founding an alliance no longer requires resources, the first players online can claim a name, gather their first members and start building a doctrine before the galaxy even fills up.

## How to get ready before launch

### Follow the countdown on Discord

The Dynasty Nova Discord relays every launch announcement, from the final countdown to the last balancing tweaks.

### Create your account before September 26

Creating your account ahead of time lets you join the universe from the very first minute, without losing time signing up while the galaxy fills with players.

## Conclusion

On September 26, 2026, Dynasty Nova isn't launching yet another beta: it's opening its first true persistent universe. An empire built there from day one starts from the same point as all the others, and what comes next depends only on each player's strategy.
