## Which ships switch drives?

In Dynasty Nova, a free space strategy game you play in your browser and on mobile, **three ships switch to a better drive on their own when propulsion research reaches the right level**. OGame players will recognize the rule: we kept it exactly as it is, with the same thresholds.

### A rule with nothing to build

The switch doesn't require any construction. As soon as the target research reaches its level, the ship adopts the new drive: its base speed and fuel consumption become that drive's, and its speed bonuses now follow the matching research.

### Fleets in flight stay as they are

A fleet that has already left keeps its arrival time. The new drive applies to the next departures.

## The three ships involved

Each ship has its built-in drive and one or two thresholds to reach.

| Ship | OGame equivalent | Built-in drive | New drive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Navette de fret | Small Cargo | Combustion Drive | Impulse Drive, level 5 |
| Frappe-orbital | Bomber | Impulse Drive | Hyperspace Drive, level 8 |
| Récupérateur | Recycler | Combustion Drive | Impulse Drive level 17, or Hyperspace Drive level 15 |

### The Navette de fret, first threshold

The Navette de fret is the first ship to switch drives, at Impulse Drive level 5. It flies much faster, but burns more hydrogen: a trade-off to keep in mind for regular transports.

### The Frappe-orbital and the Récupérateur

The Frappe-orbital gains speed at Hyperspace Drive level 8, without burning any more fuel. The Récupérateur has two thresholds, and **either one is enough on its own**: if you reach Hyperspace Drive 15 first, the Récupérateur flies with that drive straight away, even below Impulse Drive level 17.

## Where to see it in game

The drive switch is explained wherever a player might look for it.

### On the ship's sheet

A **Drive** block shows the built-in drive, then each threshold as a goal: the target research, your progress with a bar, and what the threshold unlocks in speed and fuel consumption. Each threshold has a status, **Current**, **Surpassed** or **to reach**, and one click opens the research.

### On the research pages

The sheet for a propulsion research lists, under **Unlocks**, the ships that switch to its drive and at what level, with a **Drive** badge. At the level in question, the ship is marked **Switches drive**, and any rise in fuel consumption is highlighted in color.

## What it changes for your fleets

A faster drive shortens trips, and with them the time a fleet stays exposed.

### Rethinking your research priorities

Raising Impulse Drive to level 5 speeds up all of your Navette de fret logistics in one go. [Flight time](/en/blog/fleet-flight-time-ogame-formula-dynasty-nova/) follows OGame's formula: the speed of the slowest ship sets the speed of the fleet.
