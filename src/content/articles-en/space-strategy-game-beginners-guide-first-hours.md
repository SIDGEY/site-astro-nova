## Where do you start in a space strategy game?

In a browser space strategy game like OGame or Dynasty Nova, **the first hours are all about the economy**. Every building, every ship and every research project costs resources: an empire that starts producing sooner grows faster. This guide follows the order of a new commander's decisions in [Dynasty Nova](/en/blog/free-space-strategy-browser-game-dynasty-nova/), but the habits apply to the whole genre.

### The four resources to watch

Dynasty Nova has three building resources (**metal**, **crystal** and **hydrogen**), plus the **energy** that keeps the mines running. They're shown live at the top of the screen: open a block to see the details of production and storage.

### Follow the quests

Quests always give you the next objective and reward every step. The “Go” button takes you straight to the right screen: it's the shortest way to learn the game without getting lost.

## Mines and energy

Production rests on three buildings: the **Mineral Excavator** for metal, the **Crystal Extractor** for crystal and the **Hydrogen Condenser**.

### Level up your mines evenly

Metal is the most abundant resource, and hydrogen also serves as fuel for your fleets. Level all three in parallel rather than pushing one very high: an empire held back by a single resource ends up waiting, and waiting is expensive.

### Keep an eye on your energy balance

The deeper an excavator digs, the more energy it uses. **Photovoltaic Sensors** are your first energy source; the more powerful **Thermonuclear Reactor** comes later and runs on hydrogen. Before each new mine level, check that your energy production will keep up.

### Expand storage in time

When a resource's stock reaches the planet's capacity, **its production pauses**. The Alloy Depot, the Crystal Chamber and the Hydrogen Tank raise that ceiling. If you'll be away for several hours, upgrade them before you go.

## Research and your first ships

Once production is running, two buildings open up the rest of the game.

### The Innovation Center

This lab unlocks the technologies that give access to new buildings, ships and defenses. Research keeps running while you build elsewhere: keep the lab busy.

### Espionage probes first

At the Orbital Dock, the first useful ship is the **espionage probe**, the most affordable one. Espionage is the safest mission to start with: it observes a neighboring planet with no risk to your ships, and shows you who produces what around you.

## Other players

A space strategy game is played in a shared galaxy. The first days decide who your neighbors are, and who your allies will be.

### A week of protection

In Dynasty Nova, every new player is protected from attacks for [seven days](/en/blog/new-player-protection-seven-day-shield-dynasty-nova/). The shield only drops if you attack someone yourself. Use that time to set up your economy before you enter the fray.

### Join an alliance before your shield ends

An alliance brings mutual aid, shared missions and a stronger defense than an empire on its own. Joining an alliance before your week of protection ends is the best investment a beginner can make.
