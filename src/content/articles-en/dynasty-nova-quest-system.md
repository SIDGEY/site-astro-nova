## Introduction

Building an empire takes more than lining up construction queues. Dynasty Nova now introduces a full quest campaign, with concrete objectives, rewards to claim and an interface designed never to clutter your screen.

## A campaign organized by grade tiers

### One board per grade

Quests are grouped by grade, and only one board is shown at a time. The selector works as a real filter rather than a mere scroll anchor: each grade has to be unlocked before its quests become accessible, so there's nothing to scroll through in a tier that's still locked.

### Claim your rewards right away

Each objective shows an icon taken from the main navigation, along with a clear action rather than a bare progress counter. As soon as a quest or a tier bonus becomes claimable, a dedicated entry appears in the notification center, and everything refreshes in real time without reloading the page.

## An interface designed not to clutter the screen

### Completed quests step neatly aside

On mobile, completed quests are tucked into a compact stack instead of piling up on the active board. A simple swipe lets you browse them one by one, so you never lose sight of the objectives still in progress.

### A celebration at the end of each tier

When a grade tier is fully completed, a celebration popup marks the transition before the matching bonus becomes claimable: a way to highlight a milestone actually reached rather than a mere change on a counter.

## Why it matters for your progress

Quests offer a second path of progression, alongside the usual management of resources and construction queues. They give every session a clear goal, with a visible reward and a real sense of accomplishment at each tier you clear.

**Exploring the galaxy** and opening the quest board is still the best way to find out what your current grade has already unlocked.
