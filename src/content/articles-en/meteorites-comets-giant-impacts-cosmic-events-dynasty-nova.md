## Introduction

A galaxy that never moves ends up reading like a spreadsheet. Dynasty Nova adds some texture with three **cosmic events**: meteorites, comet showers and giant impacts. They strike planets, and what they leave behind changes the map.

## Three events, three marks

### Meteorites: debris up for grabs

A meteorite impact resolves instantly. Over an inhabited planet, the fallout floats as **debris** that any player can recycle: metal, crystal and hydrogen. The owner gets a notification, and everyone else just has to send a recycler.

### Comet showers: hydrogen falls from the sky

A comet shower is the only event **announced in advance**. It targets specific galaxies and drops hydrogen. A banner above the galaxy shows the countdown and a button to each of the galaxies involved. You have time to get your fleets ready.

### Giant impacts: a planet that grows

A giant impact is rarer and longer-lasting: the planet it hits gains **one building field** for good. Sometimes the impact is more violent, adds two fields and can even form a moon. The map is never quite the same from one day to the next.

## What impacts leave on the map

### Hydrogen in the debris

Until now, a debris field held only metal and crystal. It can now hold **hydrogen** too, sometimes on its own. A position's sheet, the [orbital view](/en/blog/dynasty-nova-orbital-view/) tooltip, the deep space area and the planet's info tab all show it as soon as there is some. In the orbital view, the ring draws it as distinct shards.

### On an empty position, resources for the next colony

When an impact lands on an uninhabited position, the fallout gets buried in the ground, for the benefit of the next colony that settles there. Deep space and the Stellar Bank stations are never hit: deep space remains a safe haven.

## Stay informed without being swamped

### Four notifications, each with its own toggle

Each event has its own notification: meteorite, comet, giant impact and comet shower. It takes you straight to the galaxy where the event happened. The four toggles are grouped under **Cosmic events** in Preferences: you choose what you care about.

### Events switched on universe by universe

All three events are **off by default** and are switched on universe by universe, each with its own settings. In a universe where they're enabled, the galaxy takes on a new face: hydrogen to collect, a banner to watch, a planet that changes size.

## Conclusion

Cosmic events add the unexpected without taking away control: you see the comets coming, you choose your alerts, and every impact leaves a mark you can make the most of.
