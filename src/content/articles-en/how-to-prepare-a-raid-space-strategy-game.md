## What makes a raid profitable?

In a space strategy game like OGame or Dynasty Nova, **a profitable raid is decided before the fleet leaves**. The loot has to cover the fuel, any losses and the time your ships are tied up. Three questions are enough to make the call: what does the target have, can I win the fight, and when will I get there?

### The three-check rule

Spy, simulate, calculate. Skipping a step is a gamble: an unseen defense, a fleet that came home in the meantime or a trip longer than expected is all it takes to turn a raid into a dead loss.

## Spy on the target

Espionage is the safest mission in the game, and the foundation of every attack.

### What a report reveals

A spy probe reports what the target planet holds. The report's tier determines what it shows: in Dynasty Nova, the fleet appears from tier 3, defenses from tier 5 and research from tier 9.

### Spy right before you act

A report is a snapshot of a single moment. Fleet trips often last several hours: the target has time to bring its fleet home or spend its stockpile. Experienced players make a habit of sending a probe just before the attack sets off.

## Simulate the battle

A battle relies on random rolls: a single fight played out in your head doesn't tell you much.

### The battle simulator

In Dynasty Nova, the [battle simulator](/en/blog/battle-simulator-test-your-fleet-dynasty-nova/) starts from a spy report, replays the fight **50 times** with the game's real combat engine and gives you a victory rate, average losses and average survivors.

### What wasn't seen

A category missing from the report shows up as uncertain, never as a zero. If the defenses weren't seen, a better report beats a gamble.

## Calculate the trip

Flight time decides when you arrive, and therefore what you'll find when you get there.

### The formula

Dynasty Nova uses the [OGame formula](/en/blog/fleet-flight-time-ogame-formula-dynasty-nova/): **(3500 / s) × √(10 × d / v) + 10** seconds, where s is the speed percentage, d the distance and v the speed of the slowest ship.

### Set your arrival time

Lowering the speed makes the trip longer, but it saves hydrogen and lets you aim for a precise arrival. Cargo space matters too: the loot you bring back is capped by your fleet's cargo capacity.

## Check that the target can be attacked

Some targets are protected, and the game tells you before you send anything.

### The protections

- **Newcomer shield**: 7 days of protection from the player's arrival in the universe.
- **Beginner protection**: no attack possible when the point gap is too wide, in either direction.
- **Anti-bashing**: a cap on the number of attacks against the same player, across all their colonies.
- **Vacation mode**: an empire on pause can't be attacked.

### After the battle

A battle leaves a debris field in orbit: a Recycling mission collects its resources. To send your fleet, head to [play.dynastynova.com](https://play.dynastynova.com/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=intention-raid).
