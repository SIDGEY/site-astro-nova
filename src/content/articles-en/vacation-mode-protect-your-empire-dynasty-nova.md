## Introduction

Vacation mode is here in Dynasty Nova: the classic browser strategy pause, fully protected, but set one universe at a time. Three conditions to leave, an empire that is frozen rather than exposed, and a return you have to earn: here is what actually changes for your account.

## Taking a break, one universe at a time

### A pause that doesn't take everything offline

Vacation mode is set universe by universe, never for the whole account. You can pause one universe and keep playing normally in another the same evening: putting one galaxy to sleep no longer means shutting your eyes to all the others.

### Three conditions before you can leave

Leaving requires an empire at rest, exactly as in other games of the genre: no fleet on the move, no construction, research, shipyard or demolition order in progress, and no raid already heading your way. If any of these conditions isn't met, the refusal lists every blocker at once, not just the first one it runs into, and the Settings screen shows that same list before you even click, so the grayed-out button explains itself.

## What stops, what keeps running

### Hands off your empire, but nothing hidden

Once the pause is on, your account becomes read-only in that universe. Building, demolishing, canceling or speeding up an order, sending a fleet, firing missiles or launching a phalanx sweep: all of these actions are closed off. Viewing stays open everywhere, from the galaxy and your reports to chat and your alliance, so you can always follow what's going on without ever being able to step in.

### Frozen production, never mistaken for a breakdown

Your mines show zero production while you're away, but that line is clearly set apart from the ones that flag an energy shortage: one is a deliberate choice that lifts when you return, the other is a fault to fix, and mixing them up would push you to build power plants for nothing. Your resources neither pile up nor disappear through a fudged calculation: each base remembers the moment of its last calculation and only settles the difference when you come back, never before.

## Safe from other players

### A shield checked at the source, not just on arrival

The protection doesn't rely on a simple refusal at the moment an attack lands: the same check removes your planets from the targets shown in the galaxy, blocks a hostile fleet as soon as someone tries to send it, and keeps the game's bots from targeting you. Raids, espionage and moon destruction are ruled out before they even leave, not just rejected when they arrive.

### Coming back has a price: 48 hours, no less

You can't lift the pause before 48 hours have passed, and the refusal gives the exact moment when you'll be able to return, which feeds an on-screen countdown. On the other hand, nothing stops you from leaving again right after you come back: Dynasty Nova adds no delay between two breaks, but going back on vacation requires an empire at rest once more, which in practice leaves you exposed for as long as you're active.

## Conclusion

Vacation mode comes down to one simple promise: step away without leaving your empire exposed, and come back without losing what you've built. One universe at a time, with clear conditions for leaving and for returning, this pause protects your strategy while your life goes on elsewhere.
