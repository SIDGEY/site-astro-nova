## Introduction

A space strategy game is better with others: you share a map, you cover each other, you plan your moves together. Dynasty Nova is a free space strategy game that runs in your browser and works on mobile, where each universe brings players together in the same galaxy. Here's how to play it with your friends, from your first planet to a private universe.

## Joining the same universe

### Open universes are free

Each **universe** is a separate game, with its own map, players and ranking. Open universes are free and labeled as such when you sign up: if you all pick the same one, you share the same galaxy.

### A location drawn at random

In an open universe, the location of your first planet is drawn at random. So your friends won't necessarily be your neighbors: it's the alliance that brings you together, not your position on the map.

### Seven days to settle in

During your first seven days in a universe, nobody can attack you. That's the time to build up your economy together, as [the article on this shield](/en/blog/new-player-protection-seven-day-shield-dynasty-nova/) explains.

## Forming an alliance

### Founding without spending resources

An **alliance** brings players together under one banner. Founding one costs no resources: you only need to have researched Stellar Diplomacy. Ranks and recruitment are covered in detail in [the article on alliances](/en/blog/dynasty-nova-alliances/).

### No friendly fire

Members of the same alliance can't attack each other: nobody can target an ally's planet with their fleet or their missiles. Espionage and transport are still possible.

### A channel to coordinate

The [universe chat](/en/blog/dynasty-nova-universe-chat/) has three channels: general, alliance and private messages. The alliance channel also lets you [share coordinates or a spy report](/en/blog/alliance-chat-coordinates-spy-reports-dynasty-nova/) without leaving the game.

## Helping each other every day

### The common purse

A member in trouble can ask the alliance's common purse for help, and an officer decides on the payout. How it works is explained in [the article on member aid](/en/blog/dynasty-nova-alliance-common-purse-member-aid/).

### A shared view of the galaxy

Positions spied on by one member light up for the whole alliance: what one friend discovers, you see too. The [shared galaxy view](/en/blog/shared-galaxy-view-alliance-dynasty-nova/) turns every spy probe into the group's eyes.

## A universe all your own

### Your rules, your date

To play in a small group, the **private universe** is a paid option: you choose its size, its speeds, its rules and its opening date. Everything can still be changed until it opens.

### A code or a link to invite

Your friends join the universe with an invitation code or a link, as [the article on private universes](/en/blog/private-universe-invite-code-dynasty-nova/) details. Generating a new code invalidates the old one, without affecting the players who have already joined.

### Bots to fill out the map

The creator of a private universe can add bots, when creating it or mid-game, at an easy, intermediate or expert level. The creator can also pause or remove them.

## Conclusion

Playing Dynasty Nova with friends starts simply: the same open universe, for free, then an alliance to move forward together. The private universe is there for those who want to set their own rules. For your first hours, follow the [beginner's guide](/en/blog/space-strategy-game-beginners-guide-first-hours/).
