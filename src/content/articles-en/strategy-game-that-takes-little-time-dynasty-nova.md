## Introduction

Yes, a strategy game can be played in a few minutes a day, as long as it's designed for it. Browser-based space strategy games in the vein of OGame are the textbook example: your empire moves forward while you get on with your life. **Dynasty Nova**, a free space strategy game you play in your browser, on desktop or mobile, is played in short sessions: a few check-ins a day are enough.

## Why a browser game doesn't take much time

### The game runs without you

In a browser strategy game, the action plays out on the server, not on your screen. Your mines produce, your buildings go up and your research moves forward while you're offline. You come back to make decisions, not to wait.

### Decisions rather than hours of screen time

A Dynasty Nova session comes down to a few actions: start a building, launch a research, send a fleet, reply to your alliance. The strategy lies in the order of those choices, not in the time you spend in front of the screen.

## Planning your short sessions

### Expand your storage before you leave

When a resource's stock reaches the planet's capacity, its production pauses. Before a long day, upgrade the **Alloy Depot**, the **Crystal Chamber** or the **Hydrogen Tank**: your empire will keep producing until you're back. Our [guide to your first hours](/en/blog/space-strategy-game-beginners-guide-first-hours/) gives you the build order to follow.

### Keep the lab busy

A research in progress keeps running while you build elsewhere. Starting a research before you log off makes every absence count.

### Time your fleet to arrive when you're around

Fleet speed can be set to the exact percent. By slowing a mission down, you choose its arrival time, so your ships come home right when you next log in rather than in the middle of the night.

## Never get caught off guard

### Choose your notifications

Dynasty Nova lets you [choose your mission reports and notifications](/en/blog/mission-reports-and-notifications-dynasty-nova/), sorted into three groups: combat and espionage, fleet missions, fleet returns. You keep what calls for a reaction and leave the rest on mute.

### Safe for your first days

Every new player is protected from attacks for seven days in their universe. The shield only drops if you attack someone yourself: you can play your first week at your own pace.

### Vacation mode for long absences

If you're leaving for several days, [vacation mode](/en/blog/vacation-mode-protect-your-empire-dynasty-nova/) freezes your empire, one universe at a time. Nobody can reach you: raids and spying are refused the moment they're sent.

## Playing from your phone

### A session while you wait in line

Dynasty Nova runs in your phone's browser, with no app to install, and it's the same game as on desktop. A wait in line or your commute is enough to queue up new builds and check on your fleets.

## Conclusion

A space strategy game doesn't have to take over your evenings: it asks for a few good decisions a day, and lets you live your life in between.
