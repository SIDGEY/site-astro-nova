## Introduction

Too much metal, not enough hydrogen: every space empire runs into this imbalance. Dynasty Nova answers it with the **Stellar Bank**, a screen where your resources are exchanged for **Credits**, and where those Credits turn back into resources.

## A bank in every system

### What can you trade?

The bank handles three resources: **metal**, **crystal** and **hydrogen**. Energy can't be stored or transported, so it stays off the market. Every trade moves the rate: the more of a resource pours in, the less it's worth.

### When does it open?

The bank opens on the **universe's 7th day**. Until then, its screen simply says so. It's the same milestone as the protection for new players: the [first week](/en/blog/new-player-protection-seven-day-shield-dynasty-nova/) is for building your economy, and the bank opens after that.

## Selling your resources

### Cargo ships to the station

To sell, you choose a departure planet and the cargo. The bank only accepts **small and large cargo ships**: they fly to your system's station, one hour out and one hour back. The game automatically picks just enough cargo ships, and you can adjust the selection.

### The price is set at departure

The price of each resource is locked in **when the cargo ships leave**, not when they arrive. The Credits are paid when the ships reach the station. The bank takes a tax on sales, which the summary shows before you send: you see what you'll receive before you decide.

## Buying what you're missing

### Pay now, receive in an hour

A purchase is paid right away, at the current rate. Each resource arrives on the delivery planet one hour later, **even beyond the planet's storage capacity**. A hydrogen shortage before an expedition is fixed within an hour.

### A reserve with its limits

The bank can't supply an infinite amount: if its reserve falls short for a resource, that resource isn't bought and the game tells you so. Your Credit balance has no cap and doesn't decay: you can keep it for the right moment.

## Reading the rate before you act

### A chart and a history

The screen shows each resource's current rate, in Credits per 1,000 units, along with the cross rates between resources. A chart traces how it has moved over 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, and can also be viewed as a table. A history keeps track of every one of your trades, and deliveries in progress stay visible.

### A strategist's call

Sell when the rate is high, buy when it's low: the bank adds a layer of strategy to your empire. To learn how to build a solid economy before you rely on it, the [guide to your first hours](/en/blog/space-strategy-game-beginners-guide-first-hours/) lays out the order to follow.

## Conclusion

The Stellar Bank turns a surplus into an opportunity and a shortage into a simple one-hour wait. It's up to you to read the rate and pick your moment.
