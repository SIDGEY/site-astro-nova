## Introduction

Dynasty Nova is rolling out seven contextual guided tours to help new players find their way, and making a less visible but just as important change: your progress in this tutorial now belongs to your account, not to whichever browser you happen to be using that day.

## Seven guided tours, chained together

### One tour per key screen, never forced on you all at once

The tutorial is split into seven independent tours, each triggered by a specific visit rather than by one long mandatory walkthrough at the very start of the game. Your first login introduces the resource bar, the menu and access to your quests. Arriving on the Buildings screen leads straight into research. Your first quests, your first ships, your fleet and your first alliance visit each trigger their own tour, at the moment the matching screen becomes relevant.

### Never the same screen twice, never an empty screen

A tour never triggers twice for the same player, and never on a screen that has nothing to show either: no base built yet, no lab, no shipyard. A player who wants to see everything in one go can also move straight from one tour to the next, with the game lining up the next one automatically instead of leaving them to find it.

### A bubble that shows you the element, not just a window

Each step relies on a halo placed directly on the relevant element of the screen, surrounded by a veil that dims the rest of the interface without ever blurring the highlighted element itself. A hand-drawn arrow connects the explanation bubble to that element, so there's no doubt about what it points to, on mobile and desktop alike.

## The tutorial belongs to the player, not the browser

### No more starting over when you switch devices

Before this change, tutorial progress was stored in the browser itself: a player guided on their phone found themselves guided all over again on their computer, and clearing the cache reset everything. That progress now lives on your player account, just like your appearance, so it's shared across all your devices. What the browser had already recorded is carried over once, during the switch, so no player who was already well into the tutorial has to see it all again when the update goes live.

### Turn off, resume, replay: three controls in the settings

A dedicated toggle lets you turn the tutorial off without erasing your progress: the tours you've seen stay seen, and only the ones never shown stop appearing. Turning it back on picks up exactly where you left off. A separate button, "Replay tutorial", resets your progress on the server and switches the toggle back on at the same time: no one asks to replay something they've chosen to keep switched off.

## Conclusion

This rollout brings two separate improvements together: guided tours that finally speak to the screen itself rather than to an isolated window, and a memory of your progress that truly follows you, and not whatever device happens to be in your hands that day.
