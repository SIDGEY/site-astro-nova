## Introduction

Until recently, Dynasty Nova's ranking showed the same generic gray icon for every player, human or AI. An interface fix finally changes that.

## A ranking that gave everyone the same face

### Data that was already there, never displayed

The server was already sending the avatar and AI status of every ranked player. The interface, however, ignored that information and showed the same generic avatar on every row, whoever the player was.

### An extra robot badge that wasn't clear enough

AI players were only flagged by a small robot badge next to their username, on top of the generic avatar already shown for everyone. The distinction was subtle and easy to miss at a glance.

## What changes in the ranking

### Each player's real avatar

Each row now shows the avatar the player actually uses in the ranked universe, whether it's their default portrait or a custom avatar bought in the Shop.

### A dedicated icon for AI players

Bots no longer wear a human avatar: they show a dedicated icon instead, which fully replaces the old badge next to the username rather than adding to it.

## Why it matters for your strategy

A readable ranking means spotting at a glance who's a real rival and who's an AI, without hunting for a tiny badge in a corner of the screen. It's an interface detail, but one that makes a concrete difference in how you read your rivalries.

**Exploring the galaxy** and checking the ranking is still the best way to see the difference the next time you log in.
