## Introduction

The Stellar Points shop is getting a new presentation. The three packs now read as a real progression, with a reworked entry pack and advantages that are calculated rather than hard-coded into the interface.

## A reworked entry pack

### The Discovery Pack replaces the old €0.49 pack

The old entry pack, €0.49 for 25 Stellar Points, becomes the Discovery Pack: €1.99 for 100 points. The old price had dropped below the minimum amount Stripe accepts, which simply made it impossible to sell.

### A description that no longer touts an outdated price

The pack's description has been completely rewritten around its 100 points and what they let you do, instead of still touting an "affordable price" on a pack whose price has quadrupled.

## Three packs, read as a progression

### An order and bonuses that never lie

The packs are now displayed from cheapest to most expensive, in the same order on mobile and on desktop. Each bonus shown (+10%, +20%) is calculated from each pack's actual price against the Discovery Pack rather than hard-coded: a price adjustment updates the pitch automatically, with no risk of it ending up lying about what's being sold.

### Quantity first, name second

On packs paid for with real money, the number of Stellar Points now comes before the pack's commercial name, since that's what players compare first. Packs paid for with Stellar Points, on the other hand, keep their title on top, as what they contain is a list of resources rather than a single number.

### Badges on the image, not above it

The Expansion Pack now carries a "Most popular" badge, and the Conquest Pack a "Best value" badge. Each badge sits directly on the pack's thumbnail rather than in a banner above the card, so the packs that carry one don't get their image pushed down, which would break the alignment of the row.

## A fine-tuned sales pitch

### Percentages no longer measured against the euro

The bonuses shown are no longer presented as points per euro, but simply as extra points compared with the Discovery Pack. The price in euros, for its part, is still shown on every paid pack, now right on the image rather than in a separate box.

### What still needs aligning

The Stripe payment page still shows the pack's technical tier ("standard") rather than its commercial name, because the server-side catalog doesn't carry a name for it. That adjustment is still to be handled separately.

## Conclusion

Three packs, a clear order, bonuses that recalculate themselves: the Stellar Points shop trades its fixed pitch for a progression that stays honest with every price change.
