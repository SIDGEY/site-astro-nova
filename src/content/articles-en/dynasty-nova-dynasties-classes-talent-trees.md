## An identity for every empire

With dynasties, classes and talent trees, **no two Dynasty Nova empires play the same way anymore**. Players pick a dynasty, a character and a class, then spend talent points that steer their economy, their fleet or their intelligence. The core of the game doesn't change: resources, technologies and fleets remain the foundation of any strategy.

### Two dynasties, two stories

**The Heritage** brings together humans, the augmented, cyborgs and clones, under one motto: “No one gets left behind.” **The Accord** unites xenos of many species: “Attune, or tremble.” Each dynasty brings traits, such as “The Halden Rotation” (your three mines produce a little more from the very first minute) or “Listening Far” (the enemy fleet shows up one tier earlier in your spy reports). Their values are shown before you choose.

### A level you can see

An experience ring now tracks your progress, with a level-up window. The curve takes after Diablo 2's: the first levels come fast, level 50 has to be earned.

## Ten classes, ten ways to play

Each class carries one specific strategic lever rather than a general bonus.

![The emblems of Dynasty Nova's ten classes](/uploads/blog/dynasty-nova-emblemes-des-dix-classes.webp)

### Economy and research

- **Builder**: the yard and the mine, producing more and building faster.
- **Energetician**: energy, one lever that drives all three of your mines at once.
- **Theorist**: the lab before the yard, and the expansion that follows from it.
- **Symbiote**: your planets' temperature and fields become assets.

### Fleet, defense and intelligence

- **Admiral**: what your ships are worth once they face the enemy.
- **Navigator**: speed, fuel and cargo hold, to get there first.
- **Veteran**: defenses that survive, recover and strike back.
- **Shadow**: see into other empires, stay unseen in your own.
- **Oracle**: see raids coming, and slip away from them.
- **Archivist**: lost knowledge, wrecks, debris and cargo all come back to you.

## Two talent trees

Talents are split between two trees, each with its own pool of points.

### The common tree

The common tree has three branches, **Prosperity, Exploration and Logistics**, on a 3 × 10 grid. It gets one point per level, up to 50. It sets your empire's pace: production, expeditions, movement.

### The class tree

Each class has its own 3 × 9 tree, fed by your class mastery, up to 25 points. Talents can be reset, builds can be saved, and a class change only empties this tree. In every branch, the first two rows never push the same lever: every point is a real choice.

## Talents that steer without overpowering

A poorly balanced talent system quickly turns a strategy game into a stat race. We set limits right from the design stage.

### A measured cap in combat

Between evenly matched forces, a combat branch can't add more than **+12%**, and tests verify that limit. Fleet speed bonuses are capped too. The best fleet is still the one that arrives at the right moment, with the right intel.

A patient player growing an economy, a raider who strikes fast, a strategist who knows before everyone else: each class gives you a direction, and it's up to you to combine it with your dynasty.
