## Introduction

Dynasty Nova's notification center finally separates what you've seen from what you've read. The bell now only tracks whether an entry was displayed, not whether you actually read it, and a single list replaces the groupings by type.

## Two states that no longer get mixed up

### Seen, when you close the bell

Closing the bell, however you do it (the X, a click outside the panel, the Esc key, or navigating from an entry), marks everything displayed at that moment as seen. It doesn't depend on any other action.

### Read, only when the report is actually opened

A report only becomes read when its detail page actually opens. The bell and its panel never track that state, only whether entries were displayed: the two pieces of information now sit side by side instead of being merged into one.

## One list instead of separate groups

### Reports and quests mixed by recency

The notification center no longer shows reports and completed quests in two separate blocks: they now share a single list, sorted by recency and split into four periods (Unread, Today, This week, Older).

### The same structure, denser, on the Reports page

The All tab on the Reports page uses exactly the same flat, period-based structure, plus a one-line summary with counts and the relative time of each entry. The other tabs are simply filters applied to that same list, with no separate grouped view.

### Quests join the same pipeline

Completed-quest notifications now follow the same system as reports, with their own read tracking stored on the device. The quest badge that sat on the main menu is gone in favor of the bell alone, which now gathers everything that deserves the player's attention.

## Faster to read, at a glance

### One color, one mission type

Report and notification icons now follow a palette shared by mission type, the same one used on the Fleet page, rather than a color tied to victory or failure. Players learn once that amber means espionage, and that rule holds everywhere in the game.

### Consistent coordinates and previews

Every report that mentions a destination now shows it the same way (the planet name followed by its coordinates), and the fleet preview is limited to the first named ship followed by a counter, rather than one chip per ship type involved.

## Conclusion

By finally telling seen from read, and by bringing reports and quests together in a single list you can take in at a glance, Dynasty Nova makes its notification center truer to what players actually want to know: what still deserves their attention.
