## Can you join a universe that's already running without getting raided?

Yes: in Dynasty Nova, **every new commander is protected from attacks for 7 days** from their first arrival in a universe. It answers the most common fear among players of space strategy browser games: showing up after everyone else and becoming a resource farm for the empires already settled in.

### A shield counted universe by universe

The protection isn't tied to your account but to your place in each universe. The same commander can be protected in a universe they've just joined and no longer protected in another one where they've been playing for weeks. The clock starts at your first arrival, and leaving and then coming back doesn't reset it.

### A countdown that's always visible

As long as the shield holds, a badge reading “Newcomer protection · 6d 04h left” stays pinned in the game's header on every screen. It disappears on its own when time runs out. You always know how long you have left to grow your mines, start your first research and put together a fleet.

## What breaks the shield

There's only one way to lose the shield: **attacking someone yourself**. It's a strategic choice, not a trap.

### Attacks and missiles, nothing else

Sending a fleet on an Attack mission, or firing a salvo of interplanetary missiles, breaks the protection immediately and for good. Every other mission leaves it intact: spying on a neighbor, transporting resources between your planets, colonizing or heading out on an expedition won't expose you.

### A clear warning before you take the plunge

If you set up an attack while you're shielded, the mission window shows a warning in red: immediate and permanent loss of the shield, exposure to other players, protection time left. The button's label changes to “Attack and disable my shield”. It takes a single confirmation, but there's no way to do it by accident.

## What other players see

The protection is clearly visible from both sides of the galaxy, so nobody wastes a fleet or their time on an untouchable target.

### The “New player” badge

A protected planet carries a “New player” shield badge in the galaxy, on its card and in the orbital view. The attack tile is grayed out with the reason and the time left, and missile strikes are blocked the same way. AI-controlled players follow the same rule: they never plan a raid against a protected player.

### “Too strong for you”: the other protection

Dynasty Nova also keeps a beginner protection based on the point gap. Until now, a weak player looking at a much stronger one saw “Protected beginner”, which was wrong: it was their own protection blocking the attack. In that case, the galaxy now shows **“Too strong for you”**, and the refusal message simply explains that the point gap is too wide.

## Why now is the right time to jump in

A universe that opened a few days ago is a living universe: alliances are recruiting, the ranking is moving, there are neighbors to spy on. The shield gives you a week to build your economy in safety, and then it's up to you to decide when to join the fray.
