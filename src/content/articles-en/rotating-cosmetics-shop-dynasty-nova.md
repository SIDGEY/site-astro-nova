## What changes in the shop

Dynasty Nova's shop becomes a **rotating showcase**: a new selection of avatars and planet skins every day, and every week a featured showcase for the rarest pieces. On one essential point, the principle hasn't changed: you only buy looks there, never power.

### Three tabs instead of one long list

The shop is now organized into three tabs. **Shop** shows the current showcases, **Collection** gathers every cosmetic sorted by rarity, and **Credits** holds the Stellar Points. Old links to avatars or skins open the collection directly at the right spot.

### Showcases with a countdown

Each tile shows the time left before its showcase refreshes. The “New” and “In shop” ribbons flag what has just arrived, and rarity colors are the same everywhere: from Common to Legendary, your eye recognizes a piece's value before you read its name.

## Never miss the avatar you're waiting for

A rotating shop raises a simple question: what do you do if the item you want isn't on show today? The answer comes down to two tools.

### The Collection, with what you're missing

The Collection tab shows every cosmetic for a given slot, with All, Owned, Missing and My wishlist filters. An item that isn't in any showcase appears grayed out, with a “seen N days ago” note so you know whether it came by recently.

### A wishlist that gives you a heads-up

A heart on every tile adds the item to your wishlist. When the item is off the showcase, the buy button becomes “Add to my wishlist”. On the day its showcase opens, a notification shows up under the bell icon and takes you straight to the shop.

## Limited editions and packs

Two new features round out the rotation, for collectors and for players who'd rather buy in bundles.

### Limited editions don't come back

A limited edition has its own ribbon and the note “never coming back”. It's offered in one showcase only, then shows as “Limited edition, retired” in the collection. There's no heart to tap: promising a return would be a lie.

### Packs at 20% off, adjusted to your collection

A pack bundles several cosmetics side by side, with the value of its contents struck through next to its price, cut by about 20%. If you already own part of the pack, you only pay for what you're missing, pro rata, and the detail view explains it before you buy.

## Always comfort, never power

This redesign follows the line we've held from the start: **no pay-to-win**. Avatars and planet skins change how your empire looks in the ranking, the galaxy and the chat, not how it fights.

### A shop for those who love collecting

An empire is built through strategy first: resource management, technology choices, fleet timing. The rotating shop simply gives players who love collecting a reason to come back every day.
