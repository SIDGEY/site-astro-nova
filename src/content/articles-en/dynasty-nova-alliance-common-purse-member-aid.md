## Introduction

Dynasty Nova opens a new way out of the alliance common purse. Reserved so far for collective spending, it can now come to the rescue of a member in trouble. Requests, decisions and payouts follow a mechanism designed to stay a form of aid, never a disguised transfer channel.

## A single new way out of the common fund

### The principle that protects the purse

Until now, the alliance common purse followed a strict rule: nothing ever flows out of it to a member, or it would become a channel for transfers between accounts. That rule isn't being bypassed but explicitly amended to allow a single exception: aid to a member, bounded by caps and with a trace for every payout.

### A request, never a blank check

A member can never receive what's in the fund without first making a request: every payout answers a specific request, with an amount and a date, which makes even the smallest withdrawal traceable. Each member can have only one open request at a time, so the queue an officer looks at stays a list of people to help, never a pile of accumulated wishes.

## Two linked caps, and no trimming

### One cap per member, one cap for the alliance

Two daily limits apply in parallel: one per member, the other for the whole alliance, the second always being worth five times the first. A single setting is therefore enough to move both caps together, so nobody forgets one while adjusting the other. Without the Assistance network talent, these caps stay low; the talent raises both of them in the same proportion.

### A refusal rather than a partial payout

A request that would exceed the remaining cap is refused rather than partly served: paying out whatever is left would produce an amount nobody chose. This protects the member, who knows exactly how much they can still receive that day, as much as the officer, who never risks going over a limit without realizing it.

## A human makes the call

### The same permission that already runs the purse

Serving an aid request requires the permission to manage the treasury, the same one that already authorizes the alliance's collective spending. Aid is the fund's only way out toward a member: whoever can open it to pay for a station building can logically open it to help a member. The amount to pay out is pre-filled with what was requested; changing that value is still possible, but it becomes a deliberate judgment call rather than the default.

### A quarantine that finally comes into its own

A player who joins an alliance stays in quarantine for 24 hours before they can receive aid. This delay, which already existed for other reasons, now prevents anyone from joining an alliance just long enough to collect resources and then leave right away.

## Conclusion

Aid between members turns the common purse into a safety net rather than a frozen reserve, without ever reopening the door it had closed to disguised transfers. One request, one arbiter, two linked caps: enough to support an ally in trouble without ever putting the alliance's treasury at risk.
